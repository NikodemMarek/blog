## Who am I

So for all the lost souls that somehow ended up here, my name is Nikodem, I'm from [Poland](en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland). I'm studying cybersecurity at AGH University of Kraków, and also working in IT. In my free time I undertake various projects, in the past mostly [writing software](github.com/NikodemMarek/simple-3d), but recently often aimed to [make my life harder](github.com/NikodemMarek/dotfiles). It's not all about computers tho, I try to balance it with a fair amount of sport, social activities, travelling, reading, apparently writing from now on, and other side quests.

## What is this

As the name suggests this is a blog. I'll try to keep it strictly about technology I'm interested in, projects I'm working on, things I do to improve my productivity, but highly opinionated.

## Motivations

Over the last few years I've incorporated an astonishing amount of changes into my workflows, setups and even lifestyle. Right now I often struggle to remember and understand why, which introduces additional frustration when breaking something by accident, just because I forgot there was a reason behind the current state of things. I always knew that this is a problem of documentation, I've tried multiple times to create such a thing for me, but never consistently enough to make it useful. Relying on my own will to make a consistent effort to document things is clearly not working, so it's time to introduce external motivation factors, and nothing is stronger than fear of getting judged by a random person on the internet.
